# Themes

A theme is **four animations that cross the six tubes**, with lights that follow the picture: when the clock starts, while it connects, when it is connected, and at the end of a countdown. Download the file and upload it in **Files** on the page of your clock; your faces stay.

## Add yours

Make a theme in the [creator](/creator/index.html){target="_self"}, from the [command line](/making-bins), or with an [AI](/create/ai), and send it as a pull request to the folder `themes/` of the [repository](https://github.com/yayitazale/dekatron). A theme needs an author, the license `CC-BY-SA-4.0` and four animations, and the checks of the repository (`tools/check_bin.py --gallery`) must pass. Draw your own pictures: do not use characters, logos or brands you do not own.
